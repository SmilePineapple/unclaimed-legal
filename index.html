UNCLAIMED

Never lose a notification you swiped away.

Unclaimed keeps a searchable archive of the notifications your iPhone receives, filed like tags at a lost-property counter. Codes, parcels, payments and messages wait for you until you claim them.

How it works

From iOS 27, Shortcuts can run an automation whenever an app sends a notification. You add one for each app you care about, and it hands the notification to Unclaimed’s “File Notification” action. Unclaimed guides you through setup and sends a test so you know it works.

  • Free to download. The counter shows the last 48 hours.
  • A one-off £4.99 lifetime unlock gives you the full archive, a Face ID lock, export and tidy-up rules.
  • Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no servers, no analytics.
