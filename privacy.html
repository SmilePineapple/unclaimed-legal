Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version: Unclaimed collects nothing. Your archive is stored only on your iPhone, and the developer never sees it.

What Unclaimed stores

When a Shortcuts automation you set up runs the “File Notification” action, Unclaimed saves the app name, title, subtitle, message and time you pass to it. It also saves whether you have claimed or kept each tag. This data stays in the app’s private storage on your device. Unclaimed can’t read notifications any other way: it only receives what your own automations give it.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Unclaimed has no account system, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Your archive is only sent elsewhere if you choose to export it or share a tag through the iOS share sheet.

Purchases

The lifetime unlock is handled by Apple through the App Store. Unclaimed receives only a confirmation that the purchase is valid. It never sees your payment details.

Face ID and notifications

The optional Face ID lock uses Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework, and Unclaimed never receives your biometric data. Notification permission is only used for the setup test and for reminders you ask for.

Your control

  • Bin any tag, or everything, from the app at any time.
  • Set tags to be deleted automatically after 7, 30 or 90 days.
  • Deleting the app removes all its data.
  • Stop capture by turning off the automations in Shortcuts.

Children

Unclaimed is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the new version posted here.
